Frequently Asked Questions.

Still deciding? These are the questions families ask Jessica most.

Is this really free?

Yes. The training is free to watch, and the call with Jessica is free too. There is no cost and no obligation.

Do I need a certain amount of money saved to benefit from this?

Not at all. If you own a home, have kids, or have any savings or assets you want to protect, this is for you. There is no minimum. Even a paid-off car or a savings account is worth protecting.

I already have a will. Why would I need a trust?

A will still goes through probate. That means a court controls your assets after you pass away, and your family may wait, sometimes over a year, to access your accounts, your home, or anything in your name. A properly funded trust skips probate, so your family gets access right away.

Aren't trusts only for wealthy people?

A trust is for anyone who owns a home, has savings, or has kids. If you have anything to leave behind, you need a plan. Attorney fees are the barrier, not the trust itself. It protects your family whether you have $100,000 or $1,000,000 in assets.

I looked into trusts before and it felt complicated. Is this for me?

Absolutely. This is built for everyday people, not attorneys. Jessica spent 15 years as an educator before becoming a financial planner, and she explains things clearly, in plain language, with no jargon.
